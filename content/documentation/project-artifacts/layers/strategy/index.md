# Strategy / Product

Artifacts to align investment, direction and product or portfolio priorities.

## Purpose

Answer the questions:

- What important outcome do we want to achieve?
- What major change do we want to drive?
- Why is it worth investing in this?

## Artifacts

| Artifact | Description | Value | When to use | When to omit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Theme** | Cross-cutting strategic objective that groups related initiatives | Aligns product direction with organizational strategy | There are multiple initiatives, products or horizons involved | The effort is local and short-scoped |
| **Initiative** | Relevant change oriented toward a business objective, larger than an epic and smaller than a theme | Defines the "why" behind a set of epics | Requires investment, coordination or multiple epics | A single epic covers the complete change |

## Templates

- [Theme](./templates/theme) — Template for documenting a strategic theme.
- [Initiative](./templates/initiative) — Template for documenting a business initiative.

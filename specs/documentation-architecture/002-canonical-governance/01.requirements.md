# 002 — Canonical Governance

## Intent

Publish governance decisions for taxonomy, slugs, IDs, frontmatter, canonical sources and long-page classification before structural moves.

## Audience

Authors and maintainers of Flowsy DevGuide.

## Requirements

1. Confirm the primary taxonomy and audiences: Foundations, Documentation, Engineering, Quality, AI-Assisted Development and Conventions.
2. Decide physical slug convention and route compatibility strategy.
3. Establish a single non-translatable ID catalog independent of language, folder and slug.
4. Define a minimum frontmatter contract per page type.
5. Publish a canonical-source matrix and consumer profiles for cross-cutting topics.
6. Classify pages over roughly 300 lines as keep, split or relocate.
7. Do not physically move pages, configure final contextual sidebars, split long pages or fully rewrite stack profiles yet.

## Out of Scope

- Moving Discovery or Technologies trees.
- Splitting Error Handling, DDD, Minimal APIs or Vue Ecosystem.
- Full consolidation of date/time and Outbox profiles.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Each cross-cutting topic has a declared owner page.
- No template contradicts the ID catalog.
- Each long page has a recorded keep / split / move decision.
- Governance is published under Conventions for ongoing use.

# 003 — Contextual Navigation

## Intent

Let readers navigate only the relevant area through section landings and prefix-scoped sidebars before major physical moves.

## Audience

Guide maintainers and readers navigating Discovery, Technologies and related areas during transition.

## Requirements

1. Create Backend, Data and Messaging landings on then-current routes.
2. Configure sidebars by route prefix instead of one global tree.
3. Simplify top navigation to approved primary areas or stable entry points.
4. Make section maps exhaustive and add task-oriented reading paths on landings.
5. Do not yet move Documentation, Testing or Engineering to final routes; do not split pages; do not delete existing routes.

## Out of Scope

- Promoting Documentation and Quality (004).
- Reorganizing Engineering ownership (005).
- Splitting overloaded pages (006).

## Acceptance Criteria

- Every public page is reachable from its section landing.
- The sidebar shows only the active section context.
- Backend, Data and Messaging have their own entry points.
- A reader can choose a path by intent without knowing file names.

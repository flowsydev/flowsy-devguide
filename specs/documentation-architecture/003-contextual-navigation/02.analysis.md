# 003 — Analysis

## Problem

A single global sidebar shows the full site IA even when the reader is inside one area. Backend lacks `index.md` and top access lands on `backend/concepts`. Data and Messaging are nested groups without landings. Maps omit pages already in the sidebar.

## Evidence

- VitePress can scope sidebars by route prefix without moving files.
- Missing landings force readers through sibling concept pages.
- Deep templates remain hard to find without exhaustive maps and reading paths.

## Options Considered

| Option | Pros | Cons |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contextual sidebars before moves | Improves navigation with low risk | Temporary labels may still say Discovery/Technologies |
| Move and re-nav together | Final shape sooner | Harder to isolate navigation regressions |
| Keep global sidebar | No config churn | Continues to overwhelm readers |

## Decision

Create missing landings, switch to prefix sidebars and simplify the top bar where routes are stable. Preserve existing routes for later compatibility bridges.

## Risks

- Top-nav labels that anticipate final taxonomy may confuse if routes are still under Discovery/Technologies.
- Sidebar auto-collapse behavior must be preserved.
